WHO WE ARE

Your Comfort Is Our Priority

At PEREZ-HOSTEL, we believe that every student deserves a comfortable and welcoming place to stay. Our goal is to provide an environment where students can relax, concentrate on their studies and enjoy their student life.

Located in Agogo, Kumasi, Perez Hostel aims to make hostel living a pleasant experience by placing comfort, cleanliness and hospitality at the heart of what we do.
